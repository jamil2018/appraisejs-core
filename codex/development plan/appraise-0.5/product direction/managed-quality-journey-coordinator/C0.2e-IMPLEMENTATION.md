## Non-negotiable admission invariants

1. External admission has a distinct, versioned contract and durable provenance. Preserve issued managed
   authorizations and the meaning of `WorkerSpawnReceipt`; never return a no-op managed `STARTED` receipt.
2. Derive identity from authenticated transport. A client task ID, plugin name, role label, ticket or opt-in flag
   is metadata, not principal authority. Determine what the existing authentication layer actually identifies;
   do not describe shared coordinator credentials as unique human or cryptographic host identity. Any assignment
   grant must be scoped, revocable and checked server-side on every applicable operation and client path.
3. Admission atomically binds target, Journey, role, work item, immutable input/requirement revision, authorization,
   current ownership generation and lease. Enforce attempt ceilings, eligibility, cancellation/revocation and
   human prerequisites. Recheck authority when accepting new effects and results, not just at claim time.
4. Keep the `IN_PROGRESS` submission requirement. Reach that state only through validated external admission for
   the new mode. Do not accept `WORKER_REQUESTED` output or use raw SQL/status patching as an admission mechanism.
5. Canonical schemas, semantic artifact checks, immutable revisions, atomic writes and publication/human gates
   remain authoritative. Reuse them; do not create parallel Analysis storage or a second workflow engine.
6. Bind idempotency to principal, operation, assignment and payload identity. Separate authorized readback/replay
   of an already committed outcome from admission of a new effect. Specify behavior after expiry, replacement and
   revocation: historical outcomes must never authorize new writes or disclose another principal's artifacts.
7. Permit at most one active assignment per Journey, including races between managed and external modes. Fence
   old generations after explicit reconciled takeover; never adopt uncertain effects or silently extend leases.
8. Describe Appraise operation restrictions separately from the host's ambient capabilities. Verified managed
   CONTEXT/LIFECYCLE_COMMAND boundaries are runtime assertions, not simply hashes of provided context documents.
   External receipt semantics must not claim host confinement, independent review or a restricted host tool set.
