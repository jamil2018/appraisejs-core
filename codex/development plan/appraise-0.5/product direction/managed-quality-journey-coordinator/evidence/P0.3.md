- Acceptance evidence:
  - `QR-01` permits retry only when absence is proven; otherwise the dispatch remains `UNKNOWN`.
  - `QR-02` keeps a spawn without persisted process identity `UNKNOWN`. The real App Server protocol does not
    provide a durable OS birth identity; production supervision must capture PID, OS birth marker and executable
    identity outside the provider protocol before signalling a process.
  - `QR-03` and `QR-04` prohibit blind repeats after lost `thread/start` or `turn/start` acknowledgements. Without a
    persisted provider identity they remain unresolvable, which is a supported safe outcome rather than an
    exactly-once claim.
  - `QR-05` permits same-attempt resume only for the exact persisted thread and matching configuration digest. A
    mismatch refuses resume. Real-provider preservation of qualified boundaries cannot be established while
    `B0-001` leaves the original boundary unqualified.
  - `QR-06` demonstrated that closing stdin does not necessarily stop a spawned provider. The test retained the
    reported PID/birth marker and explicitly terminated the fixture after observation.
  - `QR-07` rejects a reused PID with a changed birth marker. `QR-08` rejects late output after revocation while
    retaining an audit flag for an effect that occurred. `QR-09` forbids replacement until predecessor stop is
    proven. `QR-10` deduplicates matching deliveries and rejects payload conflicts for the same identity.
