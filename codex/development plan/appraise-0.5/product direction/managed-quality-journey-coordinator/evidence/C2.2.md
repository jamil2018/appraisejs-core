Status: `verified`; this evidence does not evaluate GC2 or start C2.3-C2.7.

## Implemented boundary

C2.2 reuses the retained C0.2e/C2.1 `EXTERNAL_V1` claim, admission, specialized Analysis submission, exact replay,
lost-acknowledgement outcome read, fencing and atomic-acceptance path. It adds no workflow engine and no generic
artifact mutation API.

The packaged native-client workflow now requires the Analyzer to resolve the real assignment
`JOURNEY_REVISION` as artifact-library entry `REQUIREMENT_REVISION:<assignment.artifactId>` and compare the returned
`artifactId` and `sourceContentHash` with the assignment. The library `revisionId` remains a human-facing ordinal and
is not confused with the assignment's database revision ID.

After specialized submission, the workflow rereads the Journey and Analysis. Required questions stop external work;
answers and corrections remain append-only Appraise records. Publication, revision request and approval remain
separate exact-version facts. A later or replacement Codex task rereads durable Journey, Analysis and pending-event
state before claiming a fresh successor whose immutable inputs include the predecessor charter, question, answer and
revision feedback. `quality_journey_resume` reconstructs Appraise work only and makes no external-process liveness
claim.

The Analysis UI reports `Recorded by Appraise`, `Published for review`, and `Approved in Appraise` separately. It
does not infer that an `actor=USER` record independently proves a particular human's identity.

## Independent review and limitations

The first exact-artifact review returned `REVISE` for three material evidence/status issues: the external vertical
slice was not consolidated, assignment-to-library requirement identity was ambiguous, and the UI overstated human
provenance. Repairs added the consolidated external successor test, specified and tested the exact identity mapping,
and changed the status to `Approved in Appraise`.

A repair review found the mapping proof still constructed its own descriptor rather than consuming the real claimed
assignment. The final repair moved the library resolution into the consolidated test and uses
`claim.assignment.inputArtifacts` directly. The final transcript-independent exact-artifact review accepted the
current frozen artifact with no material findings.

Appraise records `actor=USER` but project-credential authentication does not independently attest a natural person's
identity. No installed-plugin reconnect was performed in this task, and no external process-liveness observation is
claimed. C-B03 retains broader pause/cancellation/provider-process recovery under C3.1; C-B04 remains owned by C2.3.
