## Authority-field and receipt limitations

Worker schemas are strict ([gateway](../../packages/appraisejs/src/mcp/external-worker-gateway.ts)
190–202). The eight top-level authority additions fail before payload validation, authorization or
canonical I/O. They prove only that caller-supplied extra fields are refused. They do not replace a
bound principal, lease, credential or generation, and their string value does not test numeric
staleness. Externally bound identity must be validated separately rather than treating a payload
field as the session principal.

is not one of those current-binding field names. No runtime principal-substitution claim is made.

no-effect observation. Internal errors are intentionally collapsed, so exact layer diagnostics were
not preserved by the aggregate selector test.
