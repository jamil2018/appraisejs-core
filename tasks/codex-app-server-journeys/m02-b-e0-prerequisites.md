## Conditional minimum execution envelope

The N2 plan proposes one supervisor/collector with its listener, one Codex child, one scoped MCP
child, one thread, at most one scripted capture turn and one local provider request. No additional
helpers/guest processes, tool/model responses, tool calls, redirects, retries, auth, external/native
inference or grants. Proposed acknowledgement/capture bound is 60 seconds and overall bound 180
seconds; request/descriptor evidence bounds are 1 MiB and sanitized metadata 64 KiB. These are
proposal values only. Any required helper or different tuple/bounds must be enumerated and approved
before execution; no fallback or replacement start is allowed.

The collector must preserve whole standard/Lite callable containers, namespace/schema/grammar and
correlation semantics, while excluding raw prompts/transcript/provider bodies and credential values.
A descriptor-only projection is not an unchanged whole-request transcript. Hash inspected bytes and
projected containers; mark truncation, semantic redaction or missing contracts incomplete. The first
request consumes its slot even if capture fails. No positive function/custom/Code Mode output is
emitted; exact non-success closure/HTTP behavior needs offline verification before launch.

## Stop and evidence rules

Exclusive owned 0700/0600 state, no-follow paths, exact pins, required scoped MCP catalog, effective
environment/FD/network rules and durable reservations must precede any possible start. Existing or
unowned root, symlink, unsupported enforcer, drift, auth/ambient inheritance, uncertain flush, second
request, unexpected tool/permission/auth/network/effect, timeout or ownership/cleanup uncertainty
fences the whole run. Preserve consumed intents/UNKNOWN effects; do not resend, restart, reset
budgets, signal stored PIDs or delete unresolved history.

Captured broad names may be retained as negative exposure evidence, never permission to invoke them.
A complete capture with adequate provenance can only support a later offline mapping review; it
cannot qualify B07 Analyzer routing, real provider credential separation, OS confinement or M02
completion. B14 is excluded and its asking-only exception remains unauthorized.
