# Slice 04: Validation Strategy And Scenario Review

Create one reviewable design artifact containing portfolio strategy and obligation-linked scenarios. The artifact
records techniques, layers, risks, assurance, matrix rationale, evidence expectations, limitations, and falsifiable
assertions.

Acceptance:

- MCP and UI support design prepare, propose, read, and exact approve/reject operations.
- Critique rejects uncovered obligations, assurance downgrades, duplicate scenarios, non-observable assertions,
  happy-path-only portfolios, unjustified matrices, and missing failure meaning.
- Scenario-only approval cannot bypass strategy approval.
