# Slice 07: Attribution, Outcomes, And Decision

Reconciliation seals raw observations. Failed, blocked, or inconclusive cells require attributed findings before a
target outcome or Assessment decision can be issued.

Acceptance:

- Attribution distinguishes target, Appraise, validation-design, validation-realization, environment/data, policy,
  automation, requirement ambiguity, and inconclusive causes.
- Only sealed, reviewed `TARGET_DEFECT` findings may violate obligations.
- Other causes retain `targetOutcome: not_evaluated` and route to revision, repair, or a fresh consented Assessment.
- Final decisions include `NEEDS_REVISION` and bind evidence, findings, and obligation outcomes.
